## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: hero photograph-style AI artwork for a careful natural-history field-guide website. Create a photorealistic quiet naturalist's study beside a woodland window: a blank cream field notebook with no writing or drawings, a plain pencil, an unbranded pair of compact binoculars and a simple magnifying glass on a warmly textured wooden desk. A softly blurred sunlit forest outside the open view, olive foliage shadows and honey-colored paper, fine real-world material textures, thoughtful editorial still-life photography, natural soft daylight and restrained depth of field. Wide composition suitable for center-cropping into a website hero, clear enough to read as an observation-and-study atmosphere. Do not show any animal, animal specimen, scientific plate, identification photograph, printed page, readable text, handwriting, labels, logo, brand or watermark. This is a staged AI atmosphere image, not documentary evidence or a species-identification image. No people, no hands, no collection of dead organisms, no collage, no borders.
